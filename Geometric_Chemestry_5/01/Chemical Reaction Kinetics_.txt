    2.  **Reverse reactions**: Many chemical reactions are reversible, eventually reaching an equilibrium rather than full depletion of the reactant.
    3.  **Mass transfer limitations**: In larger or poorly mixed reactors, diffusion rates could limit the reaction, leading to non-uniform concentrations.
    4.  **Complex reaction mechanisms**: The model assumes a single-step first-order reaction, ignoring multi-step pathways, catalysis, or inhibition.
